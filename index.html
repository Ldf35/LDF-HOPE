WHO WE ARE

Change begins with people.

We believe stronger people create stronger families, and stronger families create stronger communities.

Life Development Foundation is built around a simple idea: meaningful change happens when people are given knowledge, practical support, opportunities and the confidence to build better futures.

Through workshops, community initiatives, practical support and opportunities, LDF works to help people develop the skills and attitudes needed to create positive change.
