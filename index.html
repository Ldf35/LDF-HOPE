WHO WE ARE

We believe positive change starts with people.

Life Development Foundation is built around a simple idea: stronger people create stronger families, and stronger families create stronger communities.

Through knowledge, practical support, workshops, community initiatives and opportunities, LDF works to help people develop the confidence, skills and attitudes needed to build better futures.
