OUR IMPACT

Measure. Learn. Improve.

LDF believes responsible community work should be supported by transparency, learning and measurable action.

Our Approach

Impact is more than a number.

It is about what changes in people's lives and what we learn from every initiative.

02 Deliver support

Understand

Listen to communities and understand the problems that matter.

STEP 02

Act

Develop practical programmes that respond to real needs.

STEP 03

Verify

Use appropriate records and evidence to maintain accountability.

STEP 04

Improve

Use experience and information to make future programmes stronger.
