Life Development Foundation

WHO WE ARE

Developing people and communities for a better future.

Life Development Foundation is an organisation focused on encouraging positive social development through education, family empowerment, personal development and community initiatives.

We believe lasting development is not only about providing immediate assistance. It is also about helping people develop knowledge, skills, confidence and responsible attitudes that contribute to stronger communities.

OUR BELIEF
Positive communities are created when people are given opportunities to learn, grow and contribute.
