ABOUT LDF

People. Purpose. Progress.

Life Development Foundation exists to create positive and thriving communities by helping people develop knowledge, skills, confidence and responsible attitudes.

Life Development Foundation

A foundation built around human development, practical action and community connection.

Who We Are

Development begins with people.

We believe communities become stronger when individuals have access to knowledge, opportunity, support and positive relationships.

LDF therefore focuses on initiatives that help people develop themselves while contributing positively to their families and communities.

01

Our Vision

Creating a positive and thriving community involves nurturing spirituality and fostering good attitudes, alongside the acquisition of knowledge and skills.

02

Our Mission

Spreading knowledge and organizing workshops to inspire societal change, helping people find mental happiness, and fostering responsible citizens who contribute to their country.
