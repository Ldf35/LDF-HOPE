About Us

Who we are, why we exist, and where we're going

Our Identity

Life Development Foundation is an independent, non-profit organisation registered in the United Kingdom — built to serve communities across Sri Lanka with dignity, transparency, and lasting care.

We are not just another aid group. We are a bridge — connecting hearts in the UK and around the world to families in Sri Lanka who need a hand up, not just a handout.

🌍 Where We Work

  • Base: United Kingdom — governance, transparency, global outreach
  • Impact Zone: Sri Lanka — on-the-ground delivery, local partnerships, direct support
  • Reach: Open to friends, donors, and volunteers from every country

💙 Our Promise

  • ✅ 100% transparency — track every batch, every delivery
  • ✅ Local-first — partner shops & volunteers strengthen communities
  • ✅ Dignity-led — families choose consent & receive with respect
  • ✅ No hidden costs — your support goes where it matters

Our Guiding Values

🤝 Integrity

Open books, visible proof, honest impact — always.

❤️ Compassion

We see people, not numbers. Every family matters deeply.

🌱 Sustainability

Immediate relief today + stronger self-reliance tomorrow.
